      How the ball flies (what you're learning)
      Distance

      Each club has a full carry. Power scales it. Rough costs about 14%, sand about 22%, and long clubs from sand far more.

      Timing

      The green zone is your sweet spot. Outside it you lose distance and start the ball off line: early pushes right, late pulls left. The zone shrinks in rough and sand.

      Wind

      Into the wind costs about 1% per mph; downwind adds about half that. Crosswind moves the ball more the longer and higher it flies. Low shots cut wind's effect almost in half.

      Elevation

      Uphill plays longer and downhill shorter, about 1 yard per yard of rise or drop.

      Shape

      A draw curves left and rolls a bit more; a fade curves right and lands softer. Use them to work around trees or hold against a crosswind.

      Trajectory

      High shots carry trees and stop fast; low shots run out and stay under the wind. Full, high wedges onto a green can spin back.

      Greens

      Arrows point downhill. Steeper slopes have darker arrows. Putts break toward the low side and run faster downhill. The cup only takes putts moving slowly enough.

      Caddie help

      Shows the plays-like distance, a suggested club, a ghost landing spot and the putt length mark. Turn it off once you can judge them yourself.
