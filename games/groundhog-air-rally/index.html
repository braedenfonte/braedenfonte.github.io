      How to play

      Be the last groundhog flying. Shoot crates and fly over loot to collect it. Each stat stacks to level 5. Irish Pots of Gold give two upgrade levels and a power. Health upgrades raise your maximum HP. After 5 seconds without damage, recover 6 HP per second inside the safe zone. Ramon’s Healthy Lotion heals instantly. Eliminated pilots drop their held power.

      Fly back inside the ring within 10 seconds if you enter the fog.
