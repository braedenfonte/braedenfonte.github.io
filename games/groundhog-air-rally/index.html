          How to play

          Be the last groundhog flying. Shoot crates and fly over loot to collect it. Each stat stacks to level 5. The Irish Pot of Gold drops two different +3 trait boosts and a power. Health upgrades raise your maximum HP. After 5 seconds without damage, recover 6 HP per second inside the safe zone. Ramon’s Healthy Lotion heals instantly. MAJ P’s Blabber puts nearby rivals to sleep for a few seconds (a hit wakes them). Eliminated pilots drop their held power.

          Leaving the ring triggers a fog warning. After 1.5 seconds the fog starts draining health, and it drains faster as the circle shrinks.

          Computer · WASD to fly, mouse to aim and fire, E to use a power, 1–4 to emote.
          Phone · Left stick to fly, right stick to aim and fire, Power button to activate, 💬 to emote.
